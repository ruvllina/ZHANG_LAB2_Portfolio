About Me

I have always been interested in visual communication and the way images can tell stories without using many words. Growing up, I enjoyed taking photographs of everyday moments and creating small visual projects with friends and family.

One experience that influenced my interest in design was a class where I first learned how visual choices, typography and composition could change the way a message was understood, so this made me interested in combining creativity with technology.

Today, I am interested in web design and programming because I enjoy the combination of creative thinking and problem solving. Ultimately, my future goal is to work in digital development and create useful websites and applications!
